How it works

Speak

“I made 14 thousand today, fuel 5,200, I paid owner 3,000” — in any of five languages.

Confirm

RidePass reads it back. Nothing is saved until the rider says it’s correct, and the owner confirms every payment.

Share

After 14 active days the rider gets an Ed25519-signed passport with an explainable, behaviour-only score.
