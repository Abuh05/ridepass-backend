EVERYONE · READ FIRST

Launch plan

RidePass connects six kinds of people. The order matters: organisations first, then owners, then riders.

Day 0 · Set upAdmin starts the GPU, checks status, creates keys for each agent, lessor and lender.
Day 1 · First parkAgents onboard 10–20 riders at one park and record each rider’s first voice note with them.
Days 1–14 · Daily logsRiders log every evening; owners confirm payments; lessors watch the portfolio.
Day 14+ · PassportsRiders with 14 active days get a scored passport and can share it with lenders.

PHOENIX PROGRAMME TEAM

Programme admin: onboard the organisations

  1. Start the GPU before riders log (see Starting the GPU). Then open /admin, sign in with ADMIN_API_KEY from ridepass-secrets.env, and check System status: model mode “N-ATLAS (live)”, both models “Reachable”, all four languages loaded.
  2. Create one key per field agent under “Onboard an organisation” → Type: Field-agent team, name it after the person (e.g. “Agent – Aisha, Kano park”). Separate keys mean every recording is traceable to the agent who made it.
  3. Create one key per lessor and per lender. Use the company’s legal name so riders recognise it when they are asked for permission.
  4. Hand over keys privately: in person or a direct call, never in a group chat. A key is shown once; if it is lost, create a new one.
  5. Collect every vehicle owner’s phone number from the lessors. Lessors register their own collection phone in their dashboard; agents verify independent owners in person.

Every day

  • Keep the GPU on during logging hours (17:00–23:00 WAT works well); check System status once it is up.
  • Reminders go out automatically at 19:00 Lagos time to riders who haven’t logged. “Send evening reminders now” forces them.
  • Watch First-pass accuracy and corrections by language. A language with many corrections needs a closer look.

Every week

  • Download the interactions CSV from /admin → Tools. This is your NAIC validation evidence.
  • Keep active riders under the N-ATLAS licence cap of 1,000. The console warns at 900.
Back up ridepass-secrets.env. Without DATA_KEY, riders’ encrypted names and phone numbers can’t be recovered.
AT THE PARK

Field agent: onboard a rider (about 5 minutes)

  1. Sign in at /agent with your agent key.
  2. Enter the rider’s phone number and a PIN the rider chooses (4–6 digits). Ask them to remember it; it is how they log in on the web. Tap Open rider.
  3. Language: ask which language they prefer and tap it.
  4. Consent: read the message aloud in their language. Tap the rider’s own answer. Never answer for them. If they say no, nothing is stored.
  5. Profile: type their first name, then tap vehicle (Keke / Okada / Bus) and who owns it (Own / Hire purchase / Daily rent).
  6. Owner’s phone: type the owner’s number so payments can be confirmed (send 0 if they have no owner). Daily payment: type how much they pay the owner each day.
  7. Research question: explain it simply. Keeping voice notes is optional and their name is never attached. Tap their answer.
  8. First voice note together: hand them the phone, hold the mic button while they speak today’s money, release, and check the read-back with them before tapping “Correct”.

Tell every rider before they leave

  • “Every evening, record one voice note: what you made, fuel, and what you paid the owner.”
  • “Log in at credit-passport-tau.vercel.app/rider with your phone number and PIN.” (Once WhatsApp is live, they just message the RidePass number.)
  • “If your owner gets a link, ask them to tap Yes. Confirmed payments make your passport stronger.”
  • “After 14 days of logging, send PASSPORT to get your score and a share code.”

Verify an owner in person

Open Verify an owner in person under the rider form, but only after you have seen the owner’s ID and proof they own the vehicle. Enter the owner’s phone, then hand the phone to the owner to type a 4–6 digit PIN that only they know. Tap Mark owner verified.

From then on, every payment link for that owner asks for the PIN. A rider who forwards the link can’t confirm their own payment. If the owner forgets the PIN, or it locks after 5 wrong tries, verify them again with a new PIN.

Good recordings: a quiet spot, phone 10–20 cm from the mouth, 3–10 seconds, amounts said clearly. Watch the green level bar move while they speak. If it stays flat, the microphone isn’t working.
READ THIS TO THE RIDER IF NEEDED

Rider: your daily routine

  1. Open credit-passport-tau.vercel.app/rider and log in with your phone number and PIN.
  2. Hold the green microphone and say: “I made 14 thousand today, fuel 5,200, I paid owner 3,000.” Use your own language. Let go when you finish.
  3. Check what RidePass heard. If the amounts are right, tap Correct. If not, tap Not correct and say it again. Nothing is saved until you tap Correct.
  4. If you see a link for your owner, send it to them (WhatsApp or SMS) so they can confirm your payment.

When a lessor or lender asks to see your records, RidePass asks you first. Tap Allow or Refuse. You can stop sharing any time.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Vehicle owner: confirm payments in one tap

  1. When your rider logs a payment to you, you receive a link (forwarded by the rider, or by WhatsApp once it is live).
  2. Open it. It says, in the rider’s language, “Musa says they paid you ₦3,000 on 2026-10-11. Is this correct?”
  3. Verified owners type their owner PIN. Never tell the rider your PIN; it is what proves the answer came from you.
  4. Tap Yes, correct or Not correct. A “Not correct” is shown to the rider and the payment doesn’t count.

Why owners must be verified

The link travels through the rider, so anyone holding it could tap “Yes”. That’s why a “Yes” from an unverified owner is recorded but does not count as a confirmed payment. Only answers made with the owner’s PIN, or sent from the owner’s own WhatsApp number, count.

To get verified: leasing companies register their collection phone and PIN in the dashboard (see Lessor). Independent owners meet a field agent with their ID and vehicle papers.
LEASING FIRMS & FLEET OWNERS

Lessor: track daily repayments

  1. Sign in at /dashboard with the key from the RidePass admin.
  2. Register your collection phone and a PIN (bottom of the dashboard). Payment links for that number ask for the PIN, so only your collection staff can confirm. Keep the PIN away from riders.
  3. Ask each rider for access: enter their phone, choose Daily repayment tracking, enter the daily amount due, and tap Send request. The rider must already be onboarded.
  4. The rider approves in their own chat. Tap Refresh and they appear in your portfolio.

Reading the portfolio

Paid, confirmed by owner Paid, self-reported Paid short (under 90% of due) Nothing logged
  • Alert appears after 2 days in a row without a payment. Call the rider early.
  • Arrears (est.) is the daily due minus what was reported, since the rider joined.
  • Click a rider to open their passport and run an affordability check for a new deal.
MFBs, FINTECHS & COOPERATIVES

Lender: assess a rider

  1. Get a share code from the rider: they send PASSPORT after 14 active days and read you the 8-character code. Or use Ask a rider for access → Credit passport (30 days).
  2. Redeem the code in /dashboard. The passport opens with a green “Signature & ledger verified” banner.
  3. Run an affordability check: enter the proposed daily repayment. Tick “Replaces the rider’s current owner payment” if your loan replaces their hire-purchase. You get affordable, stretch or too high, plus the maximum recommended per day.
  4. Verify independently any time at /verify using the passport JSON and the public key.
The score is behaviour-only and explainable. Use it with your own credit policy, not as the only basis for a decision. Every view you make is logged and visible to the rider.
ADMIN · DAILY

Starting the GPU (RunPod)

Voice notes need the GPU. Text messages, dashboards and passports work without it. Voice notes sent while it is off wait in the queue for up to 24 hours and are answered when it comes back. To save credit, run it during fixed daily hours (we use 08:00–20:00 WAT) and stop it overnight.

  1. In RunPod → Pods, start the pod. Check its Details show a GPU (e.g. A40 ×1): RunPod can restart a stopped pod without its GPU if someone else took it. Stopping and starting keeps the same pod ID, so nothing changes on Railway.
  2. Open Connect → Jupyter Notebook → Terminal and paste (fill in the two tokens; never share them, and never paste these lines into a chat): 
    cd /workspace && rm -rf ridepass
git clone https://github.com/Abuh05/ridepass-backend.git ridepass
cd ridepass/model-service && ./setup.sh
export HF_TOKEN=<Hugging Face token>
export MODEL_SERVICE_TOKEN=<from ridepass-secrets.env>
./start.sh
    setup.sh stops with a clear message if the pod has no GPU or too little disk, installs the tested versions, and checks the GPU works. start.sh runs both servers separately and restarts either one if it crashes.
  3. Wait 10–15 minutes, then check /admin → System status shows both models Reachable. Logs: tail -f /workspace/vllm.log /workspace/asr.log.
  4. When logging hours end, Stop the pod to stop paying for it.
If you terminate (delete) the pod, the new one has a different ID, and ASR_URL and LLM_BASE_URL on Railway must be updated.
WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
“We could not hear you” / green bar stays flatAllow the microphone (lock icon in the address bar) and choose the right mic in browser settings. Check it isn’t muted.
Stuck on “checking your voice note…”The GPU is probably off. Check /admin → System status and start the pod. The note will be processed when it’s back.
Wrong amounts in the read-backTap “Not correct” and record again more slowly in a quieter place, or type: income 14000 fuel 5200 owner 3000.
“No RidePass rider with that number”The rider hasn’t been onboarded or didn’t consent. A field agent must onboard them first.
Rider forgot their PINA field agent opens the rider with a new PIN in “Set rider PIN”.
“Building history” instead of a scoreNormal until the rider has 14 active days of logs.
An organisation lost its keyCreate a new key in /admin and give it to them. Keys cannot be shown again.
Owner: “Too many wrong PINs”The owner’s PIN is locked for 24 hours. A field agent (or the lessor, for their collection phone) can verify the owner again with a new PIN, which unlocks it right away.
Owner confirmed but the payment isn’t countedThe owner isn’t verified yet. Verify them with a PIN; future confirmations will count.
Share code “invalid or expired”Codes last 7 days. Ask the rider to send PASSPORT again for a fresh one.
